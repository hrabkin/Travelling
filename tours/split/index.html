Cover up Split fines walking through the old town in swimwear or shirtless — €150 (some reports say up to €300), enforced by communal wardens on the Riva, Marmontova and inside the Palace. Swim last, and put a shirt back on before you leave the sand.

Saturday Everything you need is open: cathedral 08:15–19:30, market 06:00–14:00, tourist info on the Riva 09:00–14:00. You dodged the Sunday problem — on Sundays the cathedral shuts visitors out until 12:30 and this whole plan would have had to run backwards.

Corfu was a fortress. Split is the opposite: a Roman emperor's retirement villa that a town moved into and never left. Diocletian built it around 300 AD; refugees from Salona took shelter inside the walls and their descendants are still there. The order matters more than the list: at 32°C you want the underground first, the tower before the heat, and the sea in the afternoon.

Tickets — buy at the Peristyle kiosk

The Palace itself is free and never closes: the streets, the four gates, the Peristyle, the Vestibule, the free cellar corridor. You only pay for four interiors — the cathedral complex (cathedral, crypt, treasury, baptistery, bell tower) and the Substructures.

Cathedral — top tier~€11–15 · includes bell tower Substructures~€8–10 · separate ticket Split in 1 dayTourist Board's own route

Prices disagree wildly across sources — €7 basic, €10–11 with the tower, €12–15 for all five, depending on who you read and which year they wrote it. So don't quote a number at the kiosk: ask for the tier that includes the bell tower and pay whatever it is. That tier also sweeps up the Temple of Jupiter (stop 5), so it's one purchase, not two. Euros since 2023, cards fine, small cash for the market. Budget ~€20 all in with the Substructures.

Why this is easier than Corfu: there is no ferry to catch. Bačvice is a walk, so the swim has no fixed ends — stretch it, trim it, or skip it if the Palace grabs you. The one real trap is the dress rule, not the clock. If you'd rather have somewhere quieter, keep walking east 5 more minutes to Ovčice — same water, a third of the people, none of the picigin.

Getting inPure walking port. Mega-ships (MSC, NCL) usually take the outer berths at Gat Sv. Duje — 10–15 min along the seafront. Smaller ships get Gat Sv. Petra, about 5. Either way: no shuttle, no tender, flat the whole way.

RouteShip → Riva → into the Palace through the Bronze Gate → out the Golden Gate → Pjaca → market → Bačvice for the swim → back along the Riva. One loop, nothing doubles back.

TicketsTwo purchases, both at the door, both at the Peristyle kiosk: the cathedral tier with the bell tower, and the Substructures. ~€20 all in.

In the bagA shirt or cover-up (the €150 rule), water, and shoes you can climb 200 open steps in. Towel and swimmers for the swim. Nothing bulky — the tower stairs are single file.

Don't miss itThe bell tower at 10:15, before the heat and the queue. And at noon the guard changes on the Peristyle with Diocletian himself — if you want that, flip stops 6 and 7 and do the Golden Gate afterwards.

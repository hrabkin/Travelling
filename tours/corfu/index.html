A relaxed self-guided day on foot through the Venetian Old Town. Tap a stop to find it on the map; tap its number to check it off as you go.

Getting inYou can walk both ways (~20–25 min each, flat and scenic) and skip buses entirely. If it's hot, take Line 16 (port ⇄ Spilia) or Line 15 (port ⇄ San Rocco) for €1.20 a ride, bought at the port kiosk.

RouteShip → Old Town on foot or Line 16 · everything inside the Old Town is walkable · back the same way.

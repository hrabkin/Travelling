Museum tickets & the €14 pass

The state combined pass is €14 (reduced €7, valid 3 days). Your plan hits just two of its sites — Old Fortress €6 + Antivouniotissa ~€4 = ~€10 — so buy singles at the door. Add the Archaeological Museum and the sum tips to ~€16, at which point the pass wins. Separate and not on the pass: the New Fortress (~€3) and the Vídos boat (~€4 return, paid on board). St Spyridon, San Giacomo and the Liston are free.

Honestly: don't pre-book. There are no timed-entry queues at these, the on-site office is cheapest (no booking fee), and the combined pass is only sold at the sites themselves. Bring cash for the Vídos boat.

Frequency — sources disagree, so confirm on the day. The island's own tourism site says roughly every 15 min, 08:15–20:30 in summer; Lonely Planet says hourly; local operators say every 30–60 min, early morning to sunset. Ask the crew for the return times as you board. The good news: swimming in the morning means even an hourly boat costs you nothing — a missed 12:45 just means the 13:45, and you'd still make the ship with time to spare.

Getting inYou can walk both ways (~20–25 min each, flat and scenic) and skip buses entirely. If it's hot, take Line 16 (port ⇄ Spilia) or Line 15 (port ⇄ San Rocco) for €1.20 a ride, bought at the port kiosk.
